Evaluate the organizing explanation of the ENTIRE draft.md separately from individual sentence readability. A reader unfamiliar with the work can explain the problem, why the chosen change addresses it, what changes in practice, and how the evidence supports the conclusion. The body selects and orders details to develop those relationships; it does not make readers reconstruct them from a migration inventory, function signatures, implementation chronology, or a list of file changes. A good introductory example followed by an inventory-led body fails. Merely replacing jargon or shortening every item while preserving that outline also fails. Necessary review or operator details may remain when their relationship to the central explanation is clear. Quote the main thread and the most important transition or missing connection; do not pass solely because every requested fact or heading appears. Preserve important limitations rather than rewarding a simpler but misleading story.
